## 2. What FeeSlayers is

Not a credit card processor. A full business development company for shop owners
and contractors. FeeSlayers takes on the growth side: getting the shop found,
getting the phone ringing, getting the customer to yes, and keeping the money
once the sale is made. Payments is one service line, and it is the last one, not
the headline.

Service lines, in the order copy should present them:

1. Google presence. Google Business Profile setup and optimization for the map
   pack, plus review generation and management. $200/month, first 30 days free
   for clients on another service.
2. Advertising and marketing. Campaigns built and run for the shop, managed
   monthly, billed per campaign.
3. Consumer financing. A lender network so customers can say yes to big-ticket
   work. Referral model: FeeSlayers sends the applicant a payment link, the
   financing company closes and follows up.
4. Payment processing. A compliant cash discount program, so the shop keeps the
   full ticket.
5. Business capital. Merchant cash advances for the shop itself, underwritten off
   business statements.

Flagship packaged offer: **The Ultimate Payment Stack, $99/month flat.** Consumer
financing plus 0% processing plus a Clover Flex terminal placed at no charge, no
lease. Google presence and advertising are quoted separately.

## 3. Audience

Auto repair shops, tire centers, collision shops, mobile mechanics, detailers.
Home services contractors as a secondary market. Owner-operators, not
enterprises. They are busy, skeptical of processors, and have been pitched
before. They care about jobs closed and money kept, not basis points.

## 4. Voice

Plain, direct, unhurried. Short sentences. Concrete nouns. Talk about the shop's
day, not the payments industry. Avoid: synergy, solutions, leverage, empower,
cutting-edge, revolutionize, seamless. Avoid stacked adjectives and exclamation
marks. Numbers beat adjectives, but only numbers that are in this file.

## 5. Color

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy | #162333 | Header, footer, primary surfaces, body text |
| Navy deep | #0E1826 | Deep sections, contrast blocks |
| Navy soft | #243449 | Secondary text on cream |
| Gold | #F0CB46 | Primary accent, main call to action |
| Gold dark | #D4AE2A | Hover and pressed states |
| Cream | #F6F5F0 | Page background |
| Cream dim | #E8E6DE | Rules, quiet fills |

Gold on navy is the brand pairing. Gold text on cream fails contrast at small
sizes, so use gold as a fill with navy text on it, not as small type.

## 6. Type

Poppins, matching existing collateral. Semibold for headings, regular for body,
medium for interface labels. One family. Body line length under 68 characters.

## 7. Verified figures

Only these may appear on the site:

- The Ultimate Payment Stack: $99/month
- Google presence: $200/month, first 30 days free for existing clients
- Financing: 35+ lender network, $1,000 to $100,000, decisions in seconds,
  funding as soon as the next business day, lender carries the default risk
- Business capital: advances from $2,500 to $250,000, 3 months in business and
  $5,000/month gross revenue to qualify, no credit score requirement, funding as
  fast as 24 hours

Quote on a call, never printed: financing setup and membership pricing, funding
fee percentage, advertising management fee per campaign.

## 8. Legal lines, verbatim

- Financing is provided by third-party lenders. Approval, terms and rates depend
  on the applicant and are set by the lender.
- FeeSlayers is not a lender and does not make credit decisions.
- Cash discount programs are implemented to comply with card brand rules and
  applicable state law.
