## FAQ Section

**Q1:** Is payment surcharging legal in Canada?
**A1:** Yes — payment surcharging has been legal in Canada since 2023, following the class action settlement between Visa/Mastercard and Canadian merchants. As of September 2023, businesses are permitted to add a surcharge to credit card transactions, provided they comply with the established rules and regulations. We ensure every program we set up is fully compliant.

**Q2:** How much can I save with a surcharge program?
**A2:** The savings depend on your payment mix — specifically what percentage of your transactions are credit card versus debit. On average, our Canadian clients save between $1,400 and $4,000 per month in net processing costs. We offer a free audit to show you your exact numbers.

**Q3:** Does this work for all business types?
**A3:** Most service-based businesses qualify. We work with auto repair shops, HVAC companies, plumbers, electricians, roofers, home renovation companies, dental practices, veterinary clinics, fitness studios, and more. If you're not sure, reach out — we'll tell you straight up.

**Q4:** What about debit cards?
**A4:** Debit card transactions are subject to different rules under the Canadian payment network regulations and cannot be surcharged. Our programs are designed to minimize the impact of debit fees on your business — and we'll show you exactly how in your custom plan.

**Q5:** What if I already have a payment processor?
**A5:** That's fine — most of our clients do. We'll review your current setup, and if a surcharge program makes sense for your business, we'll implement it alongside your existing processor. No need to switch providers.

**Q6:** Is there a contract?
**A6:** No. Our surcharge programs run month-to-month. We're confident in our results, and we don't need a long-term contract to prove it.
