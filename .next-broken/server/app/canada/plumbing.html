The problems we hear most

Challenges Plumbing businesses face every day

Emergency callouts at 2am and you're still paying processing fees

A burst pipe at 2am generates a $1,800 invoice. The homeowner pays by card. You absorb a 2.5% processing fee, which is $45 on that one job. Over a month of emergency calls, you're paying hundreds in fees on your highest-margin work. It adds up faster than most plumbers track.

No-shows on service calls cost you drive time and wages

Your plumber drove 45 minutes each way to a drain cleaning that was booked for 9am. The customer is not answering. You leave, invoice a drive fee if you have one, and move on. But you lost two hours of productive time that you cannot get back. Deposits and card-on-file policies fix this.

Estimates on re-pipes and bathroom renos get declined without financing

A $6,000 repiping job is not a cash purchase for most homeowners. Without a financing option in your pocket, you are either losing the job to a competitor who offers installments, or discounting your price to make it cash-friendly and hurting your margin.

Insurance job payouts are slow and the paper trail is painful

Water damage restoration and insurance-related work pays well but involves adjusters, waiting periods, and sometimes disputed invoices. Getting capital to bridge the gap between completing the work and receiving payment from the insurer is a real operational challenge for growing shops.

What FeeSlayer does for your plumbing business

Compliant surcharge program so you collect everything you're owed

FeeSlayer sets up a surcharge program so your customers pay the interchange fee on card transactions. You stop paying 2.4% on a $1,800 emergency call. Setup is free. We handle the bank submission and compliance. You keep more of every dollar you earn.

Consumer financing closes the big jobs

Consumer financing gives homeowners a path to pay for $4,000 to $50,000 in plumbing work over time. You present the option, the customer applies in minutes, and you get funded when the job is complete. Better close rate on large estimates. No receivables on your books.

Business capital bridges the gap on insurance jobs

CashBuoy MCAs are underwritten off your business bank statements. A $15,000 insurance job that takes 3 weeks to pay out? Get a $12,000 advance in days so you can cover labour and materials without cash-flow pressure. Keep your operation moving while the adjuster does their work.

Google Presence so you're the first plumber they call

When a pipe bursts in the middle of the night, the homeowner Googles 'emergency plumber near me.' We make sure your Google Business Profile is optimized, your reviews are strong, and your ad budget is working to capture those high-intent searches in real time.
