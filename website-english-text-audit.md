### AUTO REPAIR

**Name:** Auto Repair

**Tagline:**
> Every declined job is money left on the table.

**Description:**
> Independent auto repair shops operate on thin margins in a competitive market. Rising parts costs, technician wages, and overhead eat into profitability. Meanwhile, customers constantly compare quotes, and when they see a $300 brake job, they often delay or decline altogether.

**Pain Points:**

1. **Customers decline work because they cannot afford it upfront**
   > A $1,200 transmission repair is easy to approve when monthly payments are an option. Without financing, you're quoting against shops that offer installment plans, and you're losing jobs to someone who will put it on a credit card at 24% APR.

2. **Card processing fees quietly eat your labour margin**
   > Labour is your highest-value revenue stream, and it's also 100% card-paid. At a 2.5% processing fee on $15,000/month in labour, you're paying $375/month, or $4,500/year, for the privilege of getting paid. Over five years, that's $22,500 gone.

3. **Seasonal slowdowns hit harder when every dollar is accounted for**
   > Post-winter is when suspension and undercarriage work surges. Pre-winter is tire season. But January and February? Customers defer non-urgent work. Your fixed costs do not adjust for that. Capital and consistent lead flow are the difference between surviving and thriving Q1.

4. **No-shows and last-minute cancellations waste technician time**
   > When a customer cancels a $600 job an hour before the appointment, your technician is standing still. You're still paying wages. The solution is a booking process that requires commitment, combined with marketing that keeps your calendar full enough that gaps hurt less.

**Solutions:**

1. **Consumer financing so customers say yes to the full job**
   > Consumer financing connects your customers to 35+ lenders. Customers get approved in seconds, and funding arrives as fast as next business day. When the $300 brake quote becomes a $45/month option, your close rate goes up and customers get their cars fixed properly instead of driving on worn brakes.

2. **Compliant surcharge program: you pay nothing on card transactions**
   > FeeSlayer sets up a government-authorized surcharge program. Customers pay the interchange fee, not you. Your $375/month processing bill drops to near zero. The setup is free and takes about a week. We handle the paperwork and compliance.

3. **Google Business Profile so you show up for local searches**
   > When someone searches 'brake repair near me' or 'auto shop [city]', your shop shows up with the right info, the right hours, and reviews that build trust. We optimize your GBP, manage your reviews, and keep your Google presence working for you 24/7.

4. **Business capital to bridge seasonal gaps**
   > MCA financing through CashBuoy is underwritten off your business statements, not your personal credit score. Need $15K to cover parts inventory and payroll through a slow February? We connect you to capital so your technicians stay employed and your suppliers get paid.
